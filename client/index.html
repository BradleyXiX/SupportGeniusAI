Architecture

How it works

A dependable, document-grounded approach designed for operational clarity.

01

Connect approved knowledge

Documentation and policy texts are parsed, chunked, and embedded into a high-performance vector store with reproducible SHA-256 caching for fast, cold-start retrieval.

02

Answer with context

Every query is evaluated strictly against retrieved documentation chunks. If an inquiry falls outside approved material, the system refuses to speculate or invent unverified facts.

03

Connect verified actions

Execute client operations such as order status checks or dispatch notifications through validated endpoint webhooks. Please note: actions require client integration and verified business authorization.
